Flower in the Forest

music composition & production 2025

This project is an original composition inspired by nature. I imagined a river flowing and a beautiful flower growing in the middle of a forest by herself. I wanted to evoke a calm and wondrous atmosphere, like something about it is inviting you to explore.

I was inspired by a lot of Evan Call's music, which are cinematic orchestral soundtracks that are often involved in beautiful stories.

Lorretta Lu · Flower in the Forest

Creating this composition has strengthened my appreciation for music producers and composers. I didn't realize how something could sound so different across headphones and speakers until my own composition.

Ultimately, I am proud of my project. I created this out of love and passion for the music I've grown up with and I would love to keep doing this for fun in the future.
