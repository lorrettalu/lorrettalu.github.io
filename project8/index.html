My Fairy Wings

video game 2025

This project is an interactive visual novel and point-n-click game about a lone fairy living alone in her forest cottage after losing her kin in a past war. Over the course of one week, the player will guide the fairy through her daily routine (gardening, cooking, sleeping, and fetching water) while also getting the chance to encounter friendly woodland creatures: a bunny, squirrel, and fox. Through choices in dialogue, the player will form bonds of companionship. The narrative unfolds with cozy visuals, gentle soundscapes, and different paths, culminating in an ending of simple company or a heartfelt dinner shared with new friends. The project ultimately explores themes of hope, resilience, and finding warmth in small connections.

I focused on creating silly, whimsical, yet comforting conversations between the player and the characters. I wanted to create a cozy game that added some level of difficulty while also feeling natural and playable.
